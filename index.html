Our Story

Every spice is ground the way our grandmother ground it.

"We didn't set out to open a restaurant. We set out to share the smell of Sunday lunch."

The Spice Kitchen started in a home kitchen, with a mortar and pestle that had already crushed three generations of family recipes. Every masala we cook with today is still hand-ground the same way — roasted, cracked, and blended fresh, never bought pre-mixed.

We brought that kitchen to the Sheffield to do one thing well: cook the food we grew up on — rich curries, wok-tossed Indo-Chinese classics, and comforting dal & rice — and get it to your door while it's still hot, still fragrant, and still exactly like home.
