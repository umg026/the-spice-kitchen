About THE SPICE KITCHEN

The Spice Kitchen brings authentic, home-style flavours to Sheffield, United Kingdom, opening from 5 September 2026. We keep our menu limited on purpose — so every dish, from our fried rice and noodles to our rich veg and non-veg curries, is carefully crafted with fresh ingredients and big, bold taste. Whether it's a comforting bowl of dal rice or a sizzling plate of Chicken Manchurian, every meal is made with love and made for you.

WHY CHOOSE US

  • Limited Menu – Carefully crafted for quality, not quantity
  • Big on Taste – Authentic flavours, always fresh
  • Fast Delivery – Hot and fresh, straight to your doorstep
  • Made with Love – Every meal, made for you

Love CURRY?

From rich Paneer Butter Masala to spicy Chicken Karai, our curries are made fresh with authentic spices and served with fluffy Jeera Rice or warm Butter Naan. Comforting dal rice bowls and hearty chole chawal bring real home-style flavour to your table — good food, great mood, made for you.

CRAVING NOODLES?

Our fried rice and noodles are wok-tossed fresh to order — from classic Chicken Fried Rice to Veg Manchurian Noodles and Mushroom Rice. Big on taste, quick on delivery, and made with love, every plate is crafted to feel like a treat from home.
